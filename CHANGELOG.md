# Changelog

## Unreleased

- **Dig Dig BOOM! — Two players on one iPad:** New game now asks **Play the AI** or **Two players**. Two players sit across from each other with the iPad in portrait: each gets half the screen (the top half is turned round for the player opposite), with one Leaderboard and the **Pause** button in the middle, and no AI. Everyone digs at the same time with their own **Undo**; finishing your Dig (Bank or BOOM) waits for the other player. When both tap Continue, **one Escape Bonus roll** happens in the middle of the screen (the same number for both, only if somebody banked); then each player watches their own Leaderboard playback, opens their own Booster Pack (one shared Booster Deck: a card one player takes, the other can't) and chooses their Starter Kit at their own pace. Tap **Ready**; the next round starts when both are. A two-player game is not saved or counted toward your best game.

- **Dig Dig BOOM! — no per-battle Grit limit:** the separate cap on how much Grit you can spend in one battle is gone. You may spend all the Grit you have; the only limit is what the track holds (6).

After a BOOM the button says **To Leaderboard**. The "Your cards" panel fits on one screen without scrolling.
