# This Means WAR! — decisions

The newest rulebook ("Quickfire Kingdoms" inside) is the source. Not built yet: tunnels and the hero (special ability) cards.

- Card names: Leader / General / Warrior / Ranger / Sorcerer / Spy / Assassin (the 2022 doc says Queen/Knight/Archer, speed 1, 3 starting castles; not used).
- "Attacking card" in a weakness comparison = the higher-power card; if the lower card is its weakness, the higher card is defeated and the lower survives.
- Equal power: both cards defeated.
- Both sides running out of cards at once: nobody wins, the castle stays as it was, both armies are gone.
- Several battles in one turn: resolved in board order (the attacker's choice of order is not offered yet).
- The opponent sees how many cards are in each unit, never which. The defender's card is face up before the attacker chooses.
- Dealing a card to a castle that already holds a unit lacking that type joins that unit; otherwise it makes a new unit there (merged, duplicates discarded, at the Battle step).
- Capturing the castle holding the enemy flag wins, including by walking onto it undefended.
- Pass and Play uses a cover screen between turns and between battle choices.
- A game that nobody can finish ends as a draw after 300 turns.
