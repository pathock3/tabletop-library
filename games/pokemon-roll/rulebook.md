# Pokémon Roll! — source rules

The original designs live in Patrick's Google Drive, folder **Pokémon Roll**:
- *Pokémon Roll! Rulebook* (doc 12IcP_apu9xm3XHMhYfSDrqM0KSkmObrBRbdS2TNsxsg)
- *Pokémon Roll design doc* (doc 1TlLF1J7a0DOxtITbZctTwg6S-24KjO8xtdPZakWEoC0): the two maps, the bosses, health and attack notes, ideas not used
- *Pokémon Roll cards* (sheet 1ToSMsKffXc2Ki0SrwCo7HWqqNMmITkU39zDBZi1HiWM, tab `v2`)

The game in `index.html` follows the owner's later decisions, which override the draft rulebook; they are logged in `DECISIONS.md`. The card sheet is copied into the data block (`cardsPsv`, six faces per card plus the special move); a card changes by editing its line there.
