
- `RULING:` **5 on 5 maximum.** `rules.locationMax` is 5 (cards a side and dice). Leaders field at most five (`size` is `[5, 5]` for Blue and Lance; Lance's legendary counts as one of his five). A leader that draws more keeps the highest-level cards (ties at random, seeded) and the rest are shuffled back into their own place's draw pile at the start of the game; a beaten leader's team goes back the same way as before.
- `RULING:` **Dice by stage.** You start with one die and gain one for each *stage* of the road you have beaten a leader in (Brock; Misty or Surge; Blaine or Erika; Blue). Leaders side by side are equals: the second one gives nothing.
- `RULING:` **"Immune to <type>"** (13 cards) is now "takes 3 less damage from <type> attacks" (`rules.typeShield`), applied after weakness. "Can't be hit by < or > attacks" (Pidgeotto, Pidgeot) is unchanged.
- `RULING:` **Legendaries** are spotted by two unseen dice (a 9) as you leave the map for a place, only once you have four dice (`rules.legendaryFromSlots`); the legendary is shuffled into its home place's draw pile.

- `RULING:` **One die per card, one column at a time** (`rules.columns`). A round is every column once, left to right. For each column: tap to roll both cards' dice (the computer's stays hidden); you keep yours or reroll it (one reroll per card, plus any a card's ability adds; a face a card lets you "always reroll" is free once per die); the computer decides the same, unseen; then the column resolves — **heals, then blocks, then attacks** — and the other side's roll and move are shown. A card at 0 health still makes its move and falls at the end of the round. A card with no opponent still rolls. Cards that fall are removed and unmatched cards pair up only at the end of the round. Movement abilities (Kadabra, Celebi, Abra, Machop line, Rhyhorn/Rhydon) work before the first roll of a round only.
- `RULING:` **Coloured dice are removed** (no colours from gym leaders, no +1 for a matching colour).
- `RULING:` **Gyarados**: "Once per battle, may swap places with any ally" — once the matchups are set, before the first roll of the battle; after that it only battles.
- `RULING:` **Starmie / Staryu** ("on non-consecutive rounds, change a die to 2"): during any of your columns, after rolling, make the current die a 2. **Rattata line** ("always reroll 1s"): a 1 may be rerolled for free, once per die.
- `RULING:` **Special moves are removed** (owner's call): the multi-dice specials ("555: 6h…") no longer exist; the cards and the sheet's V3 tab have none.
- `RULING:` **Freezing a die** (Jynx "On KO, freeze an ally die"; "ice attacks freeze dice"): the card's next roll repeats the number it last had, and that die cannot be rerolled or changed (one roll). Jynx picks the ally whose last number was best (automatic); an ice attack freezes whatever it hits. Before this, ice attacks made the card lose a round.

- `RULING:` **The road** (owner's map): Brock → Misty, Lt. Surge, Power Plant. Misty → S.S. Anne and Erika. Surge → Pokémon Tower and Blaine. Erika **or** Blaine opens Safari Zone, Cinnabar Island and Blue (so Misty→Erika or Surge→Blaine both work; only Surge opens Pokémon Tower and only Misty opens the S.S. Anne). Gold lines are roads you have opened; dotted lines are roads still to come; a road that would run behind a tile arcs round it.

- `RULING:` When a leader draws more cards than they field, the highest levels stay; **at the same level a shiny is kept before the others** (a higher-level card still beats a shiny); any other tie is random.

- `RULING:` **Back to the pool of dice** (owner's call; one die per card, column by column was tried and dropped as not strategic enough): every round a die for each card, rolled together; you give out the ones you like, throw the rest once (locked in), give those out. `rules.columns` is false (the column code is kept, switched off). This replaces the one-die-per-card rulings above.
- `RULING:` **The computer never rerolls**, at any level. Easy gives its dice out at random; Normal and Master place them by thinking (Master also looks ahead).
- `RULING:` **Resolution order**: heals first; then every attack is *declared* (the other side's choices are visible); then blocks go up (you choose which card each protects, seeing the attacks); then the damage lands, in the order it was declared. A card with "attacks first" (Rapidash) still lands its damage at once. Self-damage ("1 damage to yourself") lands with the other damage. A card at 0 still makes its move and falls at the end of the round.
- `RULING:` **Starmie / Staryu** ("on non-consecutive rounds, change a die to 5"): any one of your unassigned dice becomes a 5 (not the best, but very good).

- `RULING:` **Wild fights**: the wild card is put down; you answer with one of yours; then you may tap the deck for another wild card (the computer puts it down) which you must answer, up to your dice. You can no longer put a second card down first.
- `RULING:` **The computer's rerolls** come only from its cards (a "+1 reroll", or "always reroll Ns" for free once per die); it has no reroll of its own. Easy never rerolls.
- `RULING:` **Return a card** (↩): before you press Resolve you may take one of your cards out of the fight (it goes back to your collection unharmed). Not your last card.
- `RULING:` **Pick a card**: tap, then OK in a box; if the card isn't the highest level on offer the box asks "Are you sure?".
- `RULING:` **Articuno is Ice again (weak to Fire); the Dratini family is weak to Ice** (owner).
- `RULING:` **Every move has its own element: the colour of its cell in the card sheet.** An uncoloured move is a plain Normal move, whatever the card's type; weakness, "Immune to…" shields, "<element> attacks grant…" and "dodge <element>" all look at the move's element, not the card's type. Black cells are Dark (the game's ghost). Data: the last field of each card line (`els`, six entries in sheet order).
- `RULING:` **Healing and blocking like to match**: a heal is twice as good on a card of the heal's element; a block is twice as good against an attack of the block's element. A plain (Normal) heal or block never doubles. Today no heal in the sheet is coloured, so only blocks can match.
- `OPEN:` Does a Normal block double against a Normal attack? (Not now.) Is a self-heal of the card's own element doubled? (Yes now.) Should Ghost (black) moves be immune to Normal and Fighting? (Not now; the owner said "maybe".)
- `RULING:` **The main menu is the map itself**, dealt out under the title. Continue grows the same map to full size and lights up what you have done; New Game puts Professor Oak over it (the starters, one line: only your starter levels up and evolves) and tapping a starter goes straight back to the map.
- `RULING:` **Legendaries**: from three dice, every battle at a place has a 10% chance that a legendary turns up there; it is shuffled together with the place's draw pile and discard pile into one new draw pile. (No more two-dice roll, no "spotted" message.)
- `RULING:` **Bill** (owner: "at some point Bill shows up, probably with all three evolutions"): once per game, from four dice, a battle at a place has a 10% chance to be against Bill, who has Vaporeon, Jolteon and Flareon. Beat them all and keep one; all three then leave the pool for good (a gym leader holding one just does not return it to the pool when beaten). `OPEN:` when and how often Bill should really appear, and whether it should be the Eevee line (data: `rules.bill`, `billChance`, `billFromSlots`).
- `RULING:` **Wild fights**: the place puts one down, you put one down; then, while you have dice and cards, you may put a second card down yourself (the place answers it at once) or tap the deck for it to bring one first (you must answer it). The two sides are always equal when you may choose, and the deck cannot be tapped while a card is waiting to be answered.
- `RULING:` **Return a Pokémon** is a voluntary KO: your card leaves unharmed, and if its opponent is still there you may slide a neighbour over to face it (or leave the spot empty).
- `RULING:` **Computer rerolls**: a card that gives free rerolls ("you may always reroll 1s") is used by every computer level; the computer starts each round with no rerolls of its own.
- `RULING:` **Psyduck** ("0d from cards levels 4-6") now reads "levels 30-50" (levels are named 1, 10, 20, 30, 40, 50).
- `RULING:` **Resolving a round** plays in four steps, each step all at once: heals, attacks, blocks, damage. There is no Resolve button: when every die is given out, the side bar glows and a tap on it plays the round. `OPEN:` the choices that still come up in the middle of a round (who a block covers, a dodge) are asked when their step comes, with the cards that need an answer lit.
- `RULING:` **Pokédex** records where a card was met in the wild (never in a gym fight); a Pokémon's size on its card follows its sprite's real size.
