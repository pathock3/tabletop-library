## V4 (cards and moves from the "V4" tab of the Pokémon Roll cards sheet)
- `RULING:` **Cards and moves**: all 176 cards come from V4 (columns A–I, K–P, Q). Column J ("Previously duplicate moves"), R (rules glossary) and S (design notes) are ignored. Levels 1, 10, 20, 30, 40, 50 are stored as 1–6 as before. Each move cell's colour is the move's element (green Grass, red Fire, blue Water, yellow Electric, brown Ground, purple Psychic, orange Fighting, cyan Ice, black Dark, none = Normal). **A card's move for a roll is read straight from that roll's column** (K–P = Roll of 6 … Roll of 1); a blank does nothing. `rules.moveOrder` is now `'roll'` (the old `'top'` and `'sheet'` layouts are kept only so old tests can use them). Nothing assumes a 6 is the best roll; the inverted cards just have their recoil under 6.
- `RULING:` **Keywords**: `a` attack, `p` pierce (ignores blocks and counters; type weakness still doubles it), `b` block, `c` counter, `h` heal (also removes every status), `drain`, `recoil N` (self damage, never blockable — it is the old "Nd v"), `charge` / `charge <>`, `poison`, `stun`, `freeze`. A status written with an attack ("3a, poison") rides on that attack and lands only if damage really gets through (a fully blocked, countered or soaked hit applies nothing; a counter's reflected damage never carries one). A status-only move targets the card opposite unless arrows say otherwise.
- `RULING:` **Poison**: given in round N, 1 damage at the end of round N+1 and every round after, until healed; no stacking. **Stun**: sets "loses its next turn" (the engine's `skipNext`); stunning a stunned card just keeps that, it does not stack. A heal removes poison, a pending stun and a pending freeze. **Charge**: arrives at the end of the round, is used (+1 to every number on the move per charge: attacks, pierce, heals, blocks, counters, recoil) in the next round, then is gone; a blank roll wastes it. All statuses and charges clear between battles (units are made fresh each battle).
- `RULING:` **Freeze**: at the end of the round whoever applied it chooses whether the frozen card keeps the die it just rolled next round (no reroll). When you froze it you are asked after the round (the next round will not start until you choose); the computer locks the frozen card when it rolled a 3 or less and lets a better roll go.
- `RULING:` **Ghost heals** (a heal aimed at an enemy Dark card across from the target, double as an attack) are now real attacks: blocks and counters work on them, and they cure nothing.
- `RULING:` **Mew** uses the 6 move of the card opposite; with nobody opposite it does nothing (no fallback).
- `RULING:` **Abilities** rewritten in V4 are mapped to the existing machinery where it was the same rule in new words (counters thorns-style "N pierce to the attacker", stun the attacker = lose a round, self-stuns replace "can't attack next round", and so on). New: Poliwag line (a 3 may become a 4) and Magnemite (a 4 may become a 3) and Cubone/Marowak (a 1 may become a 6, once per battle) and Paras/Parasect (+2 to one die, every other round) are the die-changing ability box on the card (tap it, then a die); Slowpoke/Slowbro lower the die of the card opposite by 3 (min 1) automatically on rounds 1, 3, 5…; Koffing's attacks all pierce; Articuno's attacks all freeze. "Takes N less damage" keeps the old rule (N is soaked once per round, any damage), now worded as a counter on the card. **Conflict**: the V4 glossary says there are no type immunities; printed "Immune to X" abilities are kept as the earlier ruling (3 less damage from that type).
- `RULING:` **Not changed** (it still works as before, and V4 does not mention it): a heal of the card's own element heals double, a counter against its own element counts double, coloured dice from gym leaders add 1, and elemental weakness doubles damage.
- Not used by any card, so only parsed: boosts that give neighbours a keyword ("+poison <>", "+pierce <>"); the plain boosts ("+2a <>", "+3h <>", "+1c <>") work as before.


## V4 ability rewrite (sheet columns I and J)
Column I is the text on the card; column J (`D.abilityDetails`, keyed by the column I text) is shown when the ability is tapped. Levels in column E are the named levels (1, 10, 20 ... 50) and are read back to ranks 1-6. Every ability is read by `D.abilities` (first match wins); `tests` checks that none is left unread.
- **Per-hit blocks** ("Has X block against each attack / ground attack / Nido ally", Magikarp's 9) cut every hit separately and are ignored by pierce (statuses still apply if damage gets through). A card's own Counters and Blocks still come after.
- **Thorns** (Sandshrew, Grimer, Omanyte...) hit the attacker after any attack that reached the card, even when the damage was blocked or countered (not when it was dodged or missed). **KO triggers** hit the card that dealt the final damage.
- **Squirtle line** counts damage its counters sent back last round (the sheet now says "countered"; this replaces the earlier "blocked or countered").
- **Free counters**: Krabby, Kingler, Larvitar and the Pupitar line give each neighbour a free 1 counter every round; Nidorino gets 1 per Nido ally. They add to any counter rolled. Tyranitar only adds +2 to a neighbour's counter move.
- **Max health** follows the cards: +1 per living Nido ally (Nidoqueen, Nidoking), +3 beside Vileplume. When it rises the card gains the same health (our reading); when it falls health drops to the new max.
- **Grass heal** (Bulbasaur line): the biggest on the team, not added up.
- **Reach** (Doduo left, Dodrio right): every move of a neighbour gets one more column on that side.
- **Swaps**: Kadabra and Celebi swap with an ally once a round, Gyarados once a battle, all between rounds before any die is given out (tap the ability, then the ally). Celebi swaps only with a card, not into an empty column. Golbat stops every column change of the other side, Tangela the ones made by abilities (all of our column changes are abilities, so they behave the same today).
- **Alakazam** and **Rhyhorn/Rhydon** decide by themselves (no prompt): Alakazam turns the attack of the card opposite onto the card of its own team that suits the defender best (not two rounds in a row); Rhyhorn swaps the card it attacked for the enemy it would most like to face.
- **Ditto** picks any card in play when round 1 begins (you tap it; the computer takes the strongest), and then *is* that card for the battle: its moves and ability, its look and element, but its own health. (Deviation: it also takes the copy's type and weakness.)
- **Haunter** keeps the earlier end-of-round yes/no flow (once per battle).
- **Hitmonchan** takes 1 recoil for each side of a "<>" punch that finds no enemy; "< or >" moves take none.
- **Rapidash** acts first and a card it knocks out does not act that round. This is the one exception to "a card at 0 still moves".
- **Hitmonlee** stays at exactly 1 health the first time it would fall (no longer protected for the rest of the round).
- **Weezing** heals 1 and the card opposite takes 1 pierce after each round if that card has more health.
- **Rerolls**: Lugia now gives your team +1 reroll (like Vulpix); "reroll any 1" and "reroll any 2 for free" are both one free reroll per die.

## Dodge, Sleep and later rulings
- **Dodge** (move keyword, `dodge` or `1a, dodge`): only the card that used it; set at the start of the round (so it protects the card whenever its own move resolves); the first attack against it misses completely (pierce too, no status, nothing triggers: no thorns, no counter, no "when it takes damage"); cleared at the round's end. Counters (and thorns, recoil, poison) are never dodged, blocked or countered.
- **Sleep** (status): always loses the next turn; at the start of each later round a die decides (4-6 wakes it and it acts that round, 1-3 it stays asleep); wakes at once if an attack hurts it (pierce too) or if it is healed; cannot be put to sleep twice; "10h, sleep" (Snorlax's Rest) sleeps itself after the heal, also when its heals have become attacks. The AI treats hitting a sleeper as a waste unless the hit knocks it out.
- **Blocks** ("Has N block against each attack", "Takes N less") only reduce attacks. Recoil, poison, thorns and damage taken for a neighbour are never reduced. Kakuna and Metapod's recoil is 1.
- A move with an element colour but no number (stun, sleep, poison, freeze, charge, dodge) has no element.
- **Gym sizes**: Brock 2 Pokémon (one out at a time, he gives you a die at the end), Misty and Lt. Surge 4 (three out at a time). A leader's signature is only shown once it has been put down in front of you, so it is learned after beating the cards in front of it, win or lose.
- **Signature entrance**: the trainer says a line (`D.sigLines`) after the signature's card has turned over.
- **Counters**: a counter soaks up to its number and sends that much back, as damage that cannot be dodged, blocked or countered.
- Not done: male/female sprites for the Pokédex — the game has no source art for them (Crystal's sheet has no gender differences).

## The 2x3 card
The owner approved the grid card from the layout lab and it is now the only card. Design calls: trading-card proportions; one element colour for the whole top half; type and weakness share the top strip (40/60); counters get ↩ and pierce 🗡 (everything else is words); main effect on the first line and riders (recoil, poison, a boost, the other half of an "or") on the second; a move that is only a number and a symbol is drawn larger and centred; empty cells are the same pale grey on every card; the ability text shrinks a little for long abilities; the rolled number's box lights up yellow in a fight (green for a number a die could go to).

## V6 (cards from the V6 tab) and the AI
- Every card's six moves come from V6 columns L-Q (nothing kept from V4); the abilities are the V4-rewrite ones (the V6 sheet did not change them). Element colours as before, plus black = Dark (white text) and grey = Dragon; an element still only counts on a move with a number. Columns T-W (move names, power) are not read.
- **pick N** ("4h pick 2"): any N of the cards on that side, wherever they are (your own cards, including itself, for heals; enemies for attacks and statuses). The player taps the N cards; the computer takes the best N. Every card that gets picked gets the whole move. Only heals, attacks and statuses use it so far (Ninetales and Chansey); blocks and counters with pick are not built because no card has them.
- **AI:** its base rerolls are 0 (the engine already gave only the player rerolls); it only has the rerolls its own cards give. Normal now plays at the old Hard strength with no bonuses (best dice for each card, recoil and blank faces weighed, good targets, careful placement and swaps). Easy is unchanged (random). Hard plays like Normal and starts every round with 1 reroll (owner's addition; the lever is `startRerolls` in `D.ai`). Normal and Hard throw away weak dice a little more readily than before (`rerollMargin` 0.25): a die on a recoil face scores low (recoil counts against it, more when HP is low), so it is the first one thrown. ", drain" after a comma is a modifier like "1a drain" (it had been dropped).
