
- `RULING:` **5 on 5 maximum.** `rules.locationMax` is 5 (cards a side and dice). Leaders field at most five (`size` is `[5, 5]` for Blue and Lance; Lance's legendary counts as one of his five). A leader that draws more keeps the highest-level cards (ties at random, seeded) and the rest are shuffled back into their own place's draw pile at the start of the game; a beaten leader's team goes back the same way as before.
- `RULING:` **Dice by stage.** You start with one die and gain one for each *stage* of the road you have beaten a leader in (Brock; Misty or Surge; Blaine or Erika; Blue). Leaders side by side are equals: the second one gives nothing.
- `RULING:` **"Immune to <type>"** (13 cards) is now "takes 3 less damage from <type> attacks" (`rules.typeShield`), applied after weakness. "Can't be hit by < or > attacks" (Pidgeotto, Pidgeot) is unchanged.
- `RULING:` **Legendaries** are spotted by two unseen dice (a 9) as you leave the map for a place, only once you have four dice (`rules.legendaryFromSlots`); the legendary is shuffled into its home place's draw pile.

- `RULING:` **One die per card, one column at a time** (`rules.columns`). A round is every column once, left to right. For each column: tap to roll both cards' dice (the computer's stays hidden); you keep yours or reroll it (one reroll per card, plus any a card's ability adds; a face a card lets you "always reroll" is free once per die); the computer decides the same, unseen; then the column resolves — **heals, then blocks, then attacks** — and the other side's roll and move are shown. A card at 0 health still makes its move and falls at the end of the round. A card with no opponent still rolls. Cards that fall are removed and unmatched cards pair up only at the end of the round. Movement abilities (Kadabra, Celebi, Abra, Machop line, Rhyhorn/Rhydon) work before the first roll of a round only.
- `RULING:` **Coloured dice are removed** (no colours from gym leaders, no +1 for a matching colour).
- `RULING:` **Gyarados**: "Once per battle, may swap places with any ally" — once the matchups are set, before the first roll of the battle; after that it only battles.
- `RULING:` **Starmie / Staryu** ("on non-consecutive rounds, change a die to 2"): during any of your columns, after rolling, make the current die a 2. **Rattata line** ("always reroll 1s"): a 1 may be rerolled for free, once per die.
- `RULING:` **Special moves are removed** (owner's call): the multi-dice specials ("555: 6h…") no longer exist; the cards and the sheet's V3 tab have none.
- `RULING:` **Freezing a die** (Jynx "On KO, freeze an ally die"; "ice attacks freeze dice"): the card's next roll repeats the number it last had, and that die cannot be rerolled or changed (one roll). Jynx picks the ally whose last number was best (automatic); an ice attack freezes whatever it hits. Before this, ice attacks made the card lose a round.

- `RULING:` **The road** (owner's map): Brock → Misty, Lt. Surge, Power Plant. Misty → S.S. Anne and Erika. Surge → Pokémon Tower and Blaine. Erika **or** Blaine opens Safari Zone, Cinnabar Island and Blue (so Misty→Erika or Surge→Blaine both work; only Surge opens Pokémon Tower and only Misty opens the S.S. Anne). Gold lines are roads you have opened; dotted lines are roads still to come; a road that would run behind a tile arcs round it.

- `RULING:` When a leader draws more cards than they field, the highest levels stay; **at the same level a shiny is kept before the others** (a higher-level card still beats a shiny); any other tie is random.
