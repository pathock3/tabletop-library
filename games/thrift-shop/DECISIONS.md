# Thrift Shop (2026) — decisions

Source: the designer's prototype spec, the later change notes (they win where they conflict), the Design Vision doc, and the Thrift Shop cards sheet, tabs **Cards 2026** and **Invites 2026**. Every tunable number is in `GAME_DATA` (the `data` script); the cards, invites and scoring rules are in the generated `sheet` script.

## Rules as built
- **Four Impressions**, always in this order: Fancy 👑, Wacky 🤪, Cute 🧸, Spooky 👻 (opposites: Fancy vs Wacky, Cute vs Spooky). Cool and Important are gone.
- **Board**: Head, Top, Bottom, Accessory (up to 4 cards played); the figure is always barefoot; empty slots show bald / plain undershirt / heart boxers / empty hand. **Hand limit 7, starting hand 0**, so a round opens with putting cards back. 7 rounds, 60-second thrift, hands carry over.
- **Two invites per round**, both visible all round; both score for every player; round Likes = A + B; both are discarded (10 of 37 per game at 5 rounds; rounds capped at 18). Each invite is ranked across all players on its own, with its own two-player line.
- **Mess**: 120 cells (15 × 8), always face down. A put-back is a confirmed action on a multi-selection (**Put back (N)**), and the cards go face down into random empty spaces. Nothing is reshuffled between rounds.
- **Scoring** follows the spec: dense ranking, tied players all get the better payout, the better of a place or a last-place payout within one block. "0 or less X gets 1 more" = `ifAtMost 0`.
- **Bots**: at the start of thrifting each bot scores its hand against both invites (best look; a sum of two Impressions is halved so pairs are not favoured), targets the better one for the round, and builds for it. It takes blind; when its hand is full it puts back, all at once and face down, every card its best look does not use. Pace 1.5–3.5 s (slow 2.5–5, fast 0.8–2).

## Calls where the notes are silent (my defaults)
- **Invite scoring rules** (`data/scoring.json`) are written by hand from each invite's "Payout (Likes)" and "Notes" text. A test cross-checks that every number in a rule is printed in the invite's text and every number in the payout line is paid by the rule. The debug view lists every invite with its text and parsed rule.
- **Rivalry**: Family Photo Day looks to the player on your **right** = the previous seat in order, wrapping; Beach Day looks to the player on your **left** = the next seat in order, wrapping. You are seat 1. At 2 players each looks at the other.
- **Invites simplified**: every invite that combines or subtracts two Impressions pays "1st 5, 2nd 2" only; there are no lower-of-two invites and no "0 or less gets 1 more" bonuses; the scoring block kinds are rank, threshold, perPoints and rivalry.
- **Golden Trophy** doubles the highest Impression only if it is above 0; a tie goes to the tied Impression named first in invite A's rule, then invite B's, then the fixed order.
- **Art** is one flat-cartoon style on one shared figure (viewBox 240 × 340), so a card's picture is the garment as worn, and the build board and reveal show the real outfit. Hand cards are stats on the left, art on the right.
- **One file, no fetch.** `data/*.json` and `data/source/*.csv` are the checked-in snapshots; the sync command (`node games/thrift-shop/tools/sync-sheet.js`) also regenerates the `<script id="sheet">` block inside `index.html`, and a test fails if they differ. Columns are read by header name.
- **Landscape.** The stage is a fixed 1180 × 820 table scaled to fit; in portrait or on a phone it is the same table smaller with a dismissible "built for a sideways iPad" strip.
- **The clock** is the one place a timer runs; it pauses while a menu is open. Everything else is player-paced.
- **Debug** (Settings): shows face-down names and the bots' hands, and unlocks the art gallery (every card's picture, a toggle to see each card on the figure, the Power tag) and the invite rules list.

## v4 (new accessories, new round structure, no prints)
- **Rules**: 5 rounds; everyone starts with **0 cards** (round 1 is a pure dig; hands carry over after); hand limit 7; thrifting timer 90 seconds; still up to 4 cards played, +1 for each bonus slot an accessory opens. Settings range: hand limit 2–7, starting hand 0–7.
- **Card backs**: the back of every card in the mess is colour-coded by type (Head blue 🎩, Top red 👕, Bottom green 👖, Accessory gold 👜; the icon is on the back too so colour is never the only cue). Setting `typeBacks`, default on, toggle in Settings. With it on, `viewFor` shows each mess cell's type (never the card) and the **bots read the backs too** (they take a type their hand lacks).
- **Prints are gone** from the data model, the importer, the accessory logic and the art (no stamp library, no `data-print`, no per-card `prints`). Garments are plain colours; the Power column (Basic / Shaper / Rule-bender / Rare, Solid on clothing) is debug-only.
- **Sheet gap**: Pinwheel's Ability cell reads `#ERROR!` (its text starts with "+", so Sheets treats it as a formula). The importer carries an override with the agreed wording ("+1 to each trait whose total, after clothing, is above 0") and fails loudly on any other `#` cell. Fix in the sheet: put an apostrophe before the "+".
- **Traits**: no accessory belongs to a trait; when one says "Choose a trait" the owner picks in the build phase (inline strip on the right when it is placed, always pre-filled with a valid default: the first trait the invites reward). Bots try every pick (and every card subset of the hand) and keep what scores best for their target invite.
- **Order of operations** (as specified): (1) clothing totals incl. bonus slots, Duct Tape, Rhinestone Brooch, Mystery Grab Bag; (2) every other bonus/penalty, each read from the step-1 snapshot (so Pinwheel is not boosted by a Mood Ring); (3) Mixtape then Reversible Tote; (4) Invisible Dog Leash; (5) Golden Trophy (doubles a positive highest; ties: invite A's first trait, invite B's, then the fixed order).
- **Clarifications used**: "clothing" = Head, Top, Bottom worn (bonus slots included); Minimalist Tote counts only the three base slots; Statement Necklace/Earrings need Head, Top and Bottom filled and every clothing card worn positive in the trait; with two cards of a type "your Top has a positive amount" is true if either does, and "Head and Top both" needs some Head and some Top; Rhinestone Brooch adds the best clothing card's amount only if above 0; Mismatched Socks counts every negative number on worn clothing; Duct Tape zeroes negatives of its trait on clothing only; Mixtape adds the first trait's total to the second; Lost and Found Box puts -3 on your own chosen trait.
- **Bonus slots** (Hatbox, Safety Pins, Spare Pants Backpack, Second Handbag): a second slot of that type exists only while the accessory is worn; taking the accessory off sends the extra card back to the hand; the cap is `maxPlayed` + 1 per bonus slot open. The figure draws the second Head stacked above the first and the second accessory in the other hand; the reveal lists any other second cards.
- **Changing Room Key**: after everyone is done and before scoring, key holders decide at the same time (one swap each of a worn card for a same-type card in hand; a swapped-in accessory gets the trait strip, then confirm). Bots take the swap that raises their target most.
- **Lucky Penny**: in any tie the owner ranks ahead (places, last place, rivalry ties, and who counts as "top" in the top-gets-zero invites); if two Penny owners tie they stay tied (only one Penny exists, so that never happens in play).
- **Mystery Grab Bag / Scratch-Off Ticket**: at the reveal flip random face-down mess cards (seeded RNG, seat order) until clothing; every flipped card (including accessories skipped on the way) is shown, then returned face down to random empty spaces. Grab Bag adds the clothing card's traits in step 1; Scratch-Off gives +4 if it has a positive amount of the chosen trait. If the mess has no clothing left, nothing happens.
